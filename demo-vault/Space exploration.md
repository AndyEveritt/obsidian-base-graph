A small vault about robotic and crewed space exploration, used to demo the **Base Graph** plugin.

- [[Missions.base|Missions]]: every mission, grouped by type.
- [[Solar system.base|Solar system]]: planets, moons and small bodies.
- [[Tasks.base|Tasks]]: plans for a lander on [[Europa]], a sample return from [[Mars]] and a rotorcraft on [[Titan]], with dependencies between tasks.
- [[Essays.base|Essays]]: longer notes that link their main subjects many times.
- [[Everything.base|Everything]]: the whole vault, grouped by folder.
